How to read the map

DFO used seabed type and water depth to model soft-shell clam habitat. Historical harvest activity established presence somewhere in a coastal sector. The regional polygons are simplified for display and cannot identify a particular clam bed. Land outlines are approximate. For harvesting decisions, check current official closures, regulations and local advisories.

Commercial landings

DFO counts commercially landed clams by the province where they came ashore. Weight is the live-weight equivalent of all species in its Clams category.

Why are the Nova Scotia bars so low in the late 2000s?

DFO published 1,885,289 kg in 2006, withheld the 2007 amount, and published 589,039 kg in 2008. Its offshore clam management plan documents vessel losses that reduced harvesting capacity in 2007–08, and identifies Grand Bank, Newfoundland and Labrador, as a port for the offshore fleet. DFO’s fishing-ground table records 19,336 tonnes of offshore clam landings from Banquereau in 2008. A catch from that bank could be unloaded outside Nova Scotia. The combined annual figures give no species, port or fleet split to explain the whole dip or the 2015 increase.
