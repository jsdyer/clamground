How to read the map

DFO used seabed type and water depth to model soft-shell clam habitat. Historical harvest activity established presence somewhere in a coastal sector. The regional polygons are simplified for display and cannot identify a particular clam bed. Land outlines are approximate. For harvesting decisions, check current official closures, regulations and local advisories.
